FAQ

How many guests can be on the 360 Photo Booth platform at one time?

Our 360 Photo Booth platform can accommodate up to four guests per recorded video, whereas our Overhead 360 Photo Booth options can accommodate 8-10 guests. In the case of larger groups, we offer the option to create multiple videos with any combination of attendees that you prefer.

Does the 360 Photo Booth platform spin or does the camera?

No need to worry about getting dizzy on our booth! The platform you stand on does not spin. The camera arm of the 360 Photo booth is the only thing spinning around your group while it records and creates your video.

What types of props do you have?

We have props to fit any event. We want to make sure everyone enjoys themselves while using our booth. At Unforgettable 360 Photo Booth LLC we understand that good props can keep everyone entertained and enjoying their time. We have bubble guns, play money, crowns, unique hats, glasses galore, boas and so much more!

What do I do on the 360 Photo Booth?

On our 360 Photo Booth you can strike your best pose, show your dance moves, and just have fun with it. There is no right way to use our booth, be creative and have fun.

Does the photo booth print pictures?

Our 360 Photo Booth records a video and once recorded it creates a video that plays forwards and in reverese creating a unique recording. A song choice chosen by the event host is also incorporated to play with the recorded video. Once recorded the videos can be airdropped immediately, or you can scan the QR Code for your video at our share station.

What is a share station?

A share station is where your videos will be played on our tv screen where it also adds a QR Code next to your video. Once the video has been uploaded to our cloud you may scan your video's QR Code and download your video to your phone from there. Once you have downloaded your video it will be shareable to friends, family, social media, or anywhere else!

Do you have something accessible for everyone?

Yes! Our Miss Aerial and Enchanted 360 Overhead enclosures do not utilize a platform. These options offers universal accessibility, ensuring everyone can partake in the unforgettable experience.
